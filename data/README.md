# `data/`

Both project owners signed a formal confidentiality agreement governing the use of this data. The data has not been and will not be shared with other members of the capstone cohort, external stakeholders beyond the primary stakeholder, or the public. No data will be provided in this repository, but we have added our initial dataset registry below to give a sense of what data is necessary to hypothetically reproduce this project.

## Dataset registry

### Dataset 1: Willamette Application Information

- **Source:** Technolutions Slate CRM (SFTP)
- **License:** Slate Applicant Data | This data is the information found on the applications of people who have applied to Willamette in the past three years (2023 - 2025 fall entry). It contains academic information, interests, demographics, and geographic location.
- **Date pulled:** 2026-06-27
- **Approximate size:** 9404 rows, 59 cols, 6.9MB
- **Owner on this project:** Seira Ramchandani
- **Where it lives in this repo:** NA
- **Ethics / consent notes:** From the raw data to the cleaned data, unnecessary columns are being removed to minimize the amount of information tied to one student that is being utilized. Prior to removal to columns, birthdate, sex, and gender were present, which in combination could lead to the identification of a person. In order to use this data, both project owners signed a confidentiality agreement. 
- **How to fetch (for a teammate cloning fresh):** Contact project owner.
