Data Exploration

This is a print-out of the columns, what they are, and what they represent.

The columns that represent our target variables are - is_nw5 - is_three_large_public - is_local_private - is_local_large_public - dest_other

These give columns are all booleans, with a 1 representing a truth and a zero representing false. Each row should have 1 out of the three columns that have a 1, and the other two columns should have a zero, since a school can either be a NW5 school, a large public school, or another type of school.

Financials

The cost to attend college can be quite large, especially going to a NW5 school instead of a large public school. This has the potential to be a large factor in where students decide to attend. It could also be interesting to look at if students are first generation and if they tend to go to a certain type of school, potentially one with less overall cost to them and their families.

Columns to look at: common_app_fee_waiver - could be null or zero even if they would receive financial aid, but they applied to schools that did not have an application fee need_based_aid - could be indicative of a person who would have to pay attention to total cost before they choose a school.

df %>% filter('need_based_aid' != 0) %>% select('need_based_aid', 'talent_scholarships', 'efc_sai', 'common_app_fee_waiver')
